# Lab 15 — Dry-Run Peer Feedback Form

**AI-Free** — your own words. Print **two copies per student** (one for each team you review), **one capture sheet and one action plan per team**, and **one set of timekeeper cards per pod**. If the instructor runs this form on Brightspace, the questions are the same.

The ratings follow the [final presentation rubric](../../project/rubrics.md#4-final-presentation--60-points), so the presenting team sees exactly where it stands before Week 16. In a 5-minute rehearsal not everything can be deep — rate whether each part is **there and clear**.

---

### A. Ratings

**4** = Excellent — clear, specific, convincing · **3** = Good — there, a bit vague · **2** = Developing — thin or confusing · **1** = Missing

| # | Rubric criterion (final points) | What to look for | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
