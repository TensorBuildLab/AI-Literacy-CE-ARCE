# Peer Evaluation and Individual Reflection

**Due:** Week 16, by the deadline posted on Brightspace (Arizona time) · **Individual** · **Weight:** 10% of the project (20 points; see the [rubric](../rubrics.md#6-individual-reflection-and-peer-evaluation--20-points)) · **Confidential:** only the instructor sees this form · **AI-Free:** write this in your own words, with no AI tools

Two parts: **A** — rate yourself and each teammate; **B** — a one-page reflection on your own learning.

How it is used:
- **Your own score** comes from doing both parts thoughtfully (see the rubric).
- **Unequal work.** If peer evaluations and team records show that one person did much less work, the instructor may lower that person's grade on the team milestones. This happens only if **at least two teammates** give that member a share (A2) **below 60% of an equal share** — below 15% in a team of 4, below 20% in a team of 3 — **and** the check-in record ("Contributions so far") agrees. The member's team-milestone points are then multiplied by a factor between 0.5 and 1.0, and the student can respond before grades are final.
- One low rating from one person does not change anyone's grade.

## Part B — Individual reflection

About **one page** (400–600 words). Answer each prompt in a short paragraph. Be honest and concrete — refer to specific moments, numbers, and decisions from your project. The rubric groups the prompts into four rows: contribution and teamwork (1, 6), technical learning (2), verification and limits (3), and responsible use and AI tools (4, 5).

1. **My contribution.** What did you personally do? Name the sections of the notebook and report you led or wrote.
2. **What I learned technically.** What is one AI or data idea you understand much better now than in Week 10? Explain it as you would to a classmate who did not take this course.
3. **Verification and limits.** Describe one moment when checking a result changed what your team believed or reported. If nothing changed, which check would you strengthen next time?
4. **Responsible use.** If a firm wanted to use your project's tool or result tomorrow, what would you tell them? Who would be accountable for decisions made with it?
5. **Working with AI tools.** How did you use AI tools on this project? Name one time an AI tool helped and one time it was wrong or misleading. How did you catch it?
6. **Teamwork.** What would you do differently in your next team project?
