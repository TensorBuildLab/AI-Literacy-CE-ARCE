## 5. When things go wrong

If someone misses meetings, misses internal deadlines, or stops replying, we will:

1. Talk to the person directly within ____ days, kindly and specifically.
2. Hold a team meeting, write down the problem and a plan, and share the notes with everyone.
3. If it is still not fixed within a week, contact the instructor together — **before Week 14**, not at the end.

The course rule for unequal work is in the [project guide](../README.md#2-teams): a member's team grade can be lowered only if at least two teammates rate their share below 60% of an equal share and the check-in record agrees.

If someone has an emergency or illness, they tell the team (no details needed) as soon as they can, and we re-divide the work.
