# Progress Check-in (Week 12 or Week 13)

**When:** a ~5-minute conversation with the instructor or TA at your laptop — **wave A:** last 25 minutes of the Week 12 lab · **wave B:** during the Week 13 lab (the wave list is posted by Sunday of Week 11) · **Upload:** this form (PDF or Markdown) + a link to your notebook on Brightspace **before your check-in lab starts** (Arizona time) · **Weight:** 10% of the project (20 points; see the [check-in rubric](../rubrics.md#2-progress-check-in--20-points)) · **AI-Assisted**

**Minimum requirement:** your **data loads** and your **baseline runs** in the notebook, with a number in real units. A first version of your main method is a bonus, not a requirement. The requirement is the same in both waves.

**Late or missed:** a late form loses 10% per day and is not accepted after 3 days (no grace days for project milestones). If you miss the conversation for an excused reason, reschedule with the instructor within one week; otherwise those points are lost.

In the conversation, be ready to: (1) run the notebook from the top, (2) show the baseline result, and (3) say what you will do next. Everyone on the team should be able to talk about the project.
